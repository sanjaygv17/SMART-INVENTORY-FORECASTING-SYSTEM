# Model Comparison

## Objective

Multiple forecasting models were evaluated to identify the most suitable approach for FMCG demand forecasting.

## Models Compared

1. ARIMA
2. LSTM
3. XGBoost

## Comparison Criteria

- Forecast Accuracy
- Training Time
- Prediction Speed
- Scalability
- Business Feature Support

## Results

ARIMA

Pros:
- Simple forecasting

Cons:
- Limited feature support
- Lower forecasting performance

---

LSTM

Pros:
- Learns sequential patterns
- Captures seasonality

Cons:
- Slower training
- Higher computational requirements

---

XGBoost

Pros:
- High accuracy
- Fast training
- Supports engineered features
- Suitable for FMCG datasets

Cons:
- Requires feature engineering

## Final Selection

XGBoost was selected as the final forecasting model for FreshMart.

The model demonstrated the best balance between forecasting accuracy, training speed and business applicability.
